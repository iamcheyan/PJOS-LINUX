Game launches from NextUI with /usr/lib on ext4 and as the squashfs image
RG SP, 2026-10-14, NextUI on TF1, second slot empty, USB attached, Wi-Fi on.
Boots A1 to A3 ran build v1.3.0-11-ga2ba8c3 (/usr/lib a directory on ext4). Boots B1 to
B3 ran build v1.3.0-14-g05f61c0 (/usr/lib the squashfs image). The builds are otherwise
the same. Boot order: B, B, A, A, A, B.

Games: Alien Hominid (GBA, gpsp core, 2.5 MB zip) and Altered Beast (Mega Drive,
picodrive core, 326 KB zip).

Method
  Each boot: restart, wait for the menu, move to the game with injected button presses
  (checked against reference pictures of the menu), press A at 60 s of uptime, quit
  through the in-game menu, launch again, then the same for the second game.
  A shell script on the device writes the A press to the pad's event node and samples,
  about every 27 ms, the uptime, the read counters of the root partition, the loop device,
  the frontend partition and the whole card, and an MD5 of rows 80 to 399 of the screen.
  "minarch starts" is the process start time. "first picture" is the first sample after
  that whose screen is neither a menu frame nor black. Saved frames show it is the same
  event in all 24 launches: the first frame of the GBA game's fade-in, and the SEGA logo.
  Times are seconds after the A press, median (lowest to highest) of three boots.
  Reads and card busy time are counted up to the first picture.
  "minarch log ms" is the total startup time minarch writes to its own log.

Result
  No measurable difference. A launch reads 11.2 MiB from the frontend card and at most
  0.47 MiB from the root partition, because NextUI has already loaded the libraries that
  minarch needs. On ext4 those root reads take 30 to 50 ms of request time on the first
  launch after a boot and nothing on later launches.

  Each boot was in one of two states that moved minarch's startup by 0.13 to 0.19 s in
  all four of its launches. The image build was in the slower state in two of its three
  boots (B2, B3) and the ext4 build in one (A2), which is why the image medians look
  slightly worse. Within the same state the builds agree: first GBA launch 2.37 s against
  2.34 and 2.35 s in the faster state, 2.53 and 2.62 s against 2.52 s in the slower one.
  The state is whether SDL finds the built-in gamepad; see input-dir-time.txt.

Limits
  Three boots per build, one launch per case per boot; differences under about 0.2 s
  cannot be resolved. The first Mega Drive launch of a boot came after the GBA launches,
  so minarch and the shared libraries were already cached. The sampler keeps one of the
  four cores busy. RetroAchievements is enabled on this card, so each launch also talks
  to its server.
